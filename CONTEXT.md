# KAYES Industries

Precision superabrasives manufacturer (diamond tools) serving glass, ceramics, and semiconductor processing. The site is a B2B product catalogue and inquiry funnel, not an e-commerce store.

## Language

### Market

**Industry**:
A market vertical we sell into (e.g., Architectural Glass, Automotive Glass, Solar Photovoltaic Glass).
_Avoid_: Sector, vertical, segment

**Application**:
A concrete end-use or finished product within an Industry (e.g., shower cubicle, office partition, windshield, solar panel).
_Avoid_: Use-case, example, imagery

**Compatible Machine**:
A CNC or processing machine platform our tools run on (e.g., Bavelloni, Bottero, Indian Made Machines).
_Avoid_: Client, customer machine, partner

### Product Domain

**ToolFamily**:
A logical grouping of sellable tools by function and bond (e.g., Diamond Wheels, Sintered Drills, Polishing Wheels, Resin Wheels).
_Avoid_: Category, group, type

**WheelProfile**:
An ISO-standard grinding wheel shape code with an engineering drawing and dimensional parameters (e.g., 6A2, 11A2, 1FF1).
_Avoid_: Shape, model, ISO type

**Product**:
A sellable SKU that implements a WheelProfile in a specific bond, grit, and dimension.
_Avoid_: Tool, item, wheel (ambiguous)

**ToolPhoto**:
A reference photograph mapping a ToolFamily or Product to its physical appearance, sourced from WhatsApp assets.
_Avoid_: Image, asset, picture

### Identity & Content

**Facility**:
The physical plant and industrial shed imagery representing manufacturing capability.
_Avoid_: Factory render, plant photo

**Executive Statement**:
The crafted copy replacing "Our commitment is measured in microns" to emphasize tolerances, durability, and guarantee without misusing microns as a measure of effort.
_Avoid_: Slogan, tagline, motto (when referring to this specific statement)
